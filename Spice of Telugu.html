About Us

At Spice of Telugu, we celebrate the fiery spirit, deep-rooted traditions, and rich culinary heritage of the Telugu heartland. From the tangy warmth of freshly ground gongura and the bold kick of Guntur chilies to slow-simmered family gravies cooked in traditional style, every recipe honors generations of homestyle cooking. We source authentic spices and farm-fresh ingredients to recreate the warmth of a festive feast served on a fresh banana leaf. Whether you are savoring our slow-cooked Natu Kodi Pulusu or enjoying the comforting richness of Gutti Vankaya, every bite is a journey through the authentic tastes of Andhra and Telangana.
